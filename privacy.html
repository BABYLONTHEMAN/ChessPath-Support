Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

What ChessPath is

ChessPath is a chess opening explorer and training extension. This policy explains what data ChessPath handles and why.

Data ChessPath may process

ChessPath does not require a ChessPath account for normal opening exploration.

When Online Friend Play is used, ChessPath may process:

  • a display name chosen by the user;
  • chess moves and game state;
  • room, match, and temporary session information needed to connect players;
  • technical network information, including IP addresses, that may be processed by the hosting/network provider while delivering the multiplayer connection.

This information is used only to provide and maintain ChessPath functionality, including multiplayer sessions and related troubleshooting.

Data ChessPath does not collect for its own purposes

  • health information;
  • financial or payment information;
  • browsing history;
  • website content;
  • advertising profiles;
  • creditworthiness or lending information.

ChessPath does not inspect the pages a user visits.

Sharing and sale of data

ChessPath does not sell user data and does not use or transfer user data for purposes unrelated to its chess-opening and multiplayer functionality.

Online Friend Play currently uses hosted WebSocket infrastructure. The infrastructure provider may process limited technical network data as necessary to operate the service.

Security and retention

ChessPath is designed to use only the data needed for the requested feature. Multiplayer session information is used to operate live game sessions. Infrastructure-level logs, if any, are governed by the hosting provider's technical operation and policies.

Changes to this policy

Because ChessPath is currently in beta, this policy may be updated if the extension's features or data practices change. The current version will remain available in the official support repository.

Contact and support

For privacy questions, bug reports, or support requests, visit the ChessPath Support issue tracker.
